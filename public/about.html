EV Charger

Log your electric car charges. Free, no account needed.

Open the app

What it does

EV Charger is a small web app you can install on your phone's home screen. It replaces a messy spreadsheet for tracking home and public charging of an electric car.

  • Start and stop a charge with one tap, or log one afterwards with start/end battery %, kWh, meter readings, price and odometer.
  • Keep track of your electricity meters: the latest reading and price per kWh become defaults for the next charge.
  • See a dashboard with cost, energy, consumption per 100 km and charging efficiency.
  • Works offline. Export your data to CSV or a JSON backup at any time.

No sign-in required

Everything is stored on your device by default. You never need an account to use the app.

Optional Google Sheets sync

If you want your log on more than one phone, or shared with someone in your household, you can sign in with Google and connect a Google Sheet. The app then:

  • creates a spreadsheet in your Google account (or uses one you choose) with Vehicles, Meters and Charges tabs;
  • reads and writes your charge records in that one spreadsheet, and nothing else;
  • uses your Google email only on your device, to renew the sign-in without asking you again.

The app has no server. Your data goes straight from your browser to Google and is never sent to the developer or anyone else. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
