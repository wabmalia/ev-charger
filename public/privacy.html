Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

EV Charger (wabmalia.github.io/ev-charger) is a small, free, open source web app for logging electric car charges. It is a personal project, has no backend server, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores and where

  • On your device. Everything you enter (vehicles, meters, charges, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device unless you connect a Google Sheet.
  • In your own Google Sheet (optional). If you sign in with Google and connect a sheet, the app reads and writes your charge data in that spreadsheet, in your Google account. The developer has no copy of it and no access to it.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets: to create the EV Charger spreadsheet, and to read and write the Vehicles, Meters and Charges tabs of the spreadsheet you connect. The app only opens the spreadsheet you create or link in Settings; it does not list, read or change any other spreadsheet.
  • email: your Google account email address, kept only in your browser as a sign-in hint so the app can renew your Google session without asking you again.

The Google access token is kept in your browser's local storage and expires after about an hour. The app does not use refresh tokens.

All requests go directly from your browser to Google. Google user data is never sent to the developer or to any third party, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models. It is used only to provide the sync feature you turned on.

EV Charger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app never shares your data. If you share your Google Sheet with someone, that happens in Google under your control, and that person can see the data in the sheet.

Deleting your data

  • Press Disconnect in Settings to sign out and stop syncing.
  • Clear the site's data in your browser (or uninstall the app) to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Delete the spreadsheet in Google Drive to remove the synced copy.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) as part of serving the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact
